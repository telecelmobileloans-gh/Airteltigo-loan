FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ATMoney Speed Loans.

What is ATMoney Speed Loan?
+
Speed Loan is a mobile loan exclusively on ATMoney that allows eligible subscribers to borrow money directly into their ATMoney wallet — instantly, 24/7, without visiting a branch or providing paper documents. It is only for ATMoney wallet holders.
Who is eligible to apply?
+
You must be 18 years or older with an active ATMoney wallet. This service is exclusively for ATMoney users — regular ATMoney subscribers without a ATMoney wallet are not eligible. Active, frequent ATMoney users (those who regularly send money, buy airtime, pay bills, and transact) enjoy higher eligibility and bigger loan limits.
Who funds the Speed Loan?
+
The loan is funded by First Atlantic Bank (FAB), a fully licensed financial institution in Ghana. ATMoney Ghana facilitates access through the ATMoney mobile platform on behalf of FAB.
How does being a regular ATMoney user affect my limit?
+
Your ATMoney wallet activity directly determines your loan eligibility. The more you transact — sending money, paying bills, buying airtime, receiving payments — the higher your assessed limit becomes. Regular, consistent use of ATMoney signals financial activity and trustworthiness, unlocking access to progressively larger loan amounts over time.
How do I apply for a Speed Loan?
+
You can apply right here on this portal by clicking "Apply Now" or via USSD on your phone. Simply enter your number, choose your loan amount and period, and once approved, the funds are sent directly to your ATMoney wallet.
What fees are charged?
+
We charge a simple, transparent service fee based on the loan amount and duration. The base rate is 8.9% per month. You will always see the exact fee amount before you accept the loan. No hidden charges.
What loan durations are available?
+
You can choose repayment periods ranging from 7 days up to 6 months (180 days), depending on your eligibility and loan amount.
What will I see before accepting the loan?
+
Before you confirm, you will see a clear summary: the amount you receive, the service fee, the total amount to repay, and the exact due date.
Can I have more than one active loan?
+
Currently, you can only have one active Speed Loan at a time. Once you repay your current loan, you can immediately apply for another one.
How do I repay the loan?
+
Repayment is automatic from your ATMoney wallet on the due date. You can also make manual early repayments or partial repayments at any time through the portal or USSD without any penalty.
